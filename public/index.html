    How to play

    1. Create or join a room, then pick a team name and a co-owner.
    2. Draft a squad that fills every role slot for the game type, without spending more than your purse.
    3. Players come up one at a time. Bid or opt out before the timer runs out.
    4. Keep enough points to fill your remaining spots — a zero purse with open slots takes you out of the auction.
    5. When the auction ends, pick a captain. Leftover points can be spent on boosters.
    6. Teams then play a simulated tournament. The champion is the winner.
