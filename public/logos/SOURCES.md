# Logo sources

Processed locally to 256×256 WebP head cut-outs: background removed (existing alpha for cartoon art; rembg `isnet-general-use` for photographs), cropped tightly from hair/top of head to the chin, centred, and saved with a soft theme-colour glow. Served from `/logos/`. No hotlinking.

The project owner explicitly accepted the copyright and likeness risk of using recognisable character artwork and photographs of real people in this private friends game.
